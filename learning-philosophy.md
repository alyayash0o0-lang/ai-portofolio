# Learning Philosophy

I approach learning AI engineering with a system-building mindset.

Rather than focusing on training complex models, I prioritize:
- understanding the problem deeply
- designing clear input and output interfaces
- building reasoning logic that users can understand
- integrating AI components as part of a larger system

## Why AI Engineering?
AI systems in the real world are not isolated models.
They are products that involve:
- software architecture
- decision logic
- explainability
- reliability

My goal is to build AI tools that are:
- practical
- modular
- scalable
- user-oriented

## How I Learn
- Build first, optimize later
- Prefer simple solutions over complex ones
- Reflect through learning journals after each project
- Iterate based on clarity and usability
